# LifeOS

**Find your direction. Build a more meaningful life.**

LifeOS is an AI-powered personal development platform designed to help people who feel lost, stuck, or uncertain about their direction in life. It helps users explore their values and priorities, define meaningful goals, and translate them into small, achievable actions.

## Vision

Help people gain clarity, agency, and momentum through thoughtful reflection and practical guidance — without replacing human relationships or professional support.

Read the [Product Vision](docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md) for the problem, intended audience, and design principles.

## Planned features

- **Life Compass:** Guided reflection on personal values, life domains, and priorities.
- **Life Strategy:** Turn insights into goals and manageable action plans.
- **Daily Companion:** Brief daily planning and reflection.
- **Personal Memory:** User-controlled continuity across sessions.
- **Life Review:** Periodic review of progress, obstacles, and changing priorities.

## First milestone

The initial goal is a small web-based **minimum viable product (MVP)**: a user can complete a Life Compass exercise, choose a priority, discuss it with an AI guide, and leave with one actionable next step.

See [MVP Scope](docs/MVP_SCOPE.md) for the proposed scope and acceptance criteria.

## Project status

**Planning / concept stage.** The scope and technical decisions are provisional. No production application is implemented yet.

## Privacy and responsible use

LifeOS may involve sensitive personal reflections. The project will use fictional or synthetic data during early development, minimize collection of personal information, and prioritize user control over stored information. LifeOS is not a mental-health diagnostic or treatment service and should not present itself as a substitute for qualified professionals.

## Development

Potential stack: React frontend, Python/FastAPI backend, and PostgreSQL for persistence, subject to change during prototyping.
